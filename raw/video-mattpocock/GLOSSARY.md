# Tidy

Language for Tidy, a fictional task app, and the intro that presents it.

## Language

**Tidy**:
The fictional task app this intro presents.
_Avoid_: the product, the app (when you mean the name)

**Intro**:
The motion piece that presents Tidy, from the first frame through the end card.
_Avoid_: trailer, video, animation (as the name of the piece)

**Logo reveal**:
The opening passage of the intro, where the Tidy mark and wordmark appear.
_Avoid_: title card, opener

**Feature beat**:
One passage in the middle of the intro. Each feature beat presents a single Tidy capability.
_Avoid_: feature section, slide, chapter

**Capture**:
The Tidy capability of saving a task in one line.
_Avoid_: add, create, inbox (as the capability name)

**Arrange**:
The Tidy capability of placing tasks into today or later.
_Avoid_: sort, organize, prioritize

**Finish**:
The Tidy capability of checking a task off so the list gets quieter.
_Avoid_: complete, delete, clear (as the capability name)

**End card**:
The closing passage of the intro, where the wordmark and closing line hold.
_Avoid_: outro, slate, credits
