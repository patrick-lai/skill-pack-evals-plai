# Quay

Quay is a shared task board for one team. The installation is the team: everyone who can sign in shares the same boards.

## Language

**Member**:
A person who can sign in to this Quay, identified by email.
_Avoid_: User, account

**Board**:
A named place where the team's tasks are tracked.
_Avoid_: Project, workspace

**Task**:
A single piece of work on a board.
_Avoid_: Card, ticket, issue

**Status**:
Where a task sits. A task has exactly one status: To do, Doing, or Done.
_Avoid_: State, stage

**Column**:
The on-screen grouping of tasks that share a status.
_Avoid_: Lane, list

**Assignee**:
The member responsible for a task. A task may have no assignee.
_Avoid_: Owner, reviewer

**Due date**:
The calendar day a task is meant to be finished. A task may have no due date.
_Avoid_: Deadline
